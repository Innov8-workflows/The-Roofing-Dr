Privacy and cookies

The Roofing Dr, Stafford. Last updated 8 September 2026.

Who we are

The Roofing Dr is a roofing business based in Stafford, covering Staffordshire and the West Midlands. We are the data controller for the information described here. You can reach us on 07886 285512.

What we collect when you ask for a quote

The quote form on this site asks four questions about the work you need, and optionally your first name. When you tap to send it, that summary is placed into a WhatsApp or text message on your own phone. You choose whether to send it.

We also keep a record of the enquiry, and of taps on our phone number, WhatsApp and text links, so we know which enquiries came from this website. That record contains what you told the form and where on the page you tapped. It does not contain your phone number unless you send us a message, in which case we hold it in the same way we would if you had rung us.

Why we are allowed to hold it

We rely on legitimate interests: you contacted us about work, and we need your details to reply and to quote. We keep enquiry records for as long as we may need them for the job and any guarantee that follows, and no longer.

Who sees it

Your enquiry is stored in Google Sheets and emailed to us, and is handled by innov8 Workflows, who build and maintain this website on our behalf. It is not sold, and it is not shared with anyone else for marketing.

Cookies

Nothing that identifies you is set unless you press Accept. If you decline, or simply ignore the banner, no measurement cookies are placed at all and the site works exactly the same.

If you accept, we use:

  • Meta Pixel - tells us whether our Facebook and Instagram adverts are reaching people who go on to ask for a quote.
  • Google Analytics - tells us how many people use the quote form and where they stop, so we can make it easier to use.

Both are used to measure advertising and improve the page. You can change your mind at any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer of the home page, or by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Recording that you made a choice is stored in your browser only. It is not a tracking cookie and it is not sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Ring 07886 285512 and we will sort it. If you are not happy with how we have handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
